About Us

Founded in 2024 in Pretoria. It quickly expanded into ecommerce, offering bold, youth centric designs inspired by urban culture. Alchemist strive's to empower young people through fashion that reflects individuality and creativity. Our vision is to become Africa’s leading streetwear brand with a global online presence.
