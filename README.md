## Project Title:
### ALCHEMIST STREETWEAR
## 1. Organisation Overview
Name of organisation: ALCHEMIST STREETWEAR
History: Founded in 2024, ALCHEMIST began as a small streetwear boutique in Pretoria. It quickly expanded into ecommerce, offering bold, youth centric designs inspired by urban culture.
Mission: To empower young people through fashion that reflects individuality and creativity.
Vision: To become Africa’s leading streetwear brand with a global online presence.
Target Audience: Youth aged 16–30, digitally smart, trend driven, and socially engaged.
## 2. Website Goals and Objectives
•	Increase online sales by 25% within the first year.
•	Drive traffic through Search Engine Optimization and social media integration.
•	Build brand loyalty through community features.
KPIs: Monthly traffic and social media engagement.
## 3. Current Website Analysis
Strengths: Strong product imagery, active Instagram integration.
Weaknesses: Slow load times, limited filtering options, outdated design.
Improvements: Faster hosting, modern User Interface, advanced product filters.
## 4. Proposed Features and Functionality	
•	Homepage with a hero banner and innovative picture to give an idea of what the brand is about.
•	About Us page with background of the brand and a video.
•	Product catalogue with filters (size, price, and buy button).
•	Enquiries page to launch an enquiry about the brand.
•	Contact us page to highlight where the brand is located and how it can be contacted
## 5. Design and User Experience
•	Colour Scheme: Black, white, neon green accents/ olive green, burnt orange, sand beige, etc.
•	Typography: Aptos slab extrabold font for headings, Aptos serif for body text.
•	Layout: Grid based, mobile first design
